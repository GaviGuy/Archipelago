# Variant Sudoku

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What is Variant Sudoku?

Variant Sudoku builds on traditional Sudoku rules with unique additions.
The puzzle grid might contain double ratio hints, palindrome lines, or even fog of war.
Puzzles often begin with no numbers. You must use these new clues to find your footing.

Rather than reinvent the wheel, the client only handles the Archipelago connection. Puzzles are crowd-sourced primarily from
the Logic Masters and Cracking the Cryptic communities, and played via [Sudokupad](https://sudokupad.app/), [f-puzzles](https://www.f-puzzles.com/), or any similar browser sudoku tool.

This APWorld will likely update periodically with new puzzles, so don't expect it to merge with core any time soon.

## What does Archipelago do to Variant Sudoku?

Items grant the player:
* Access to new puzzles
* Upgrades or usable items to assist in solving or skipping puzzles
* Gradual access to the boss puzzle
* Fluff

Locations are accessed by:
* Solving puzzles (with more locations sent for harder puzzles)
* Bonuses for solving puzzles tied to progression

To reach the goal, unlock the preset boss puzzle and solve it
